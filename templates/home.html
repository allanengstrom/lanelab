HOW IT WORKS

{# crosshair / target #}

PREDICT THE OPPONENT

A model trained on past coach decisions predicts the lineup they're most likely to run, using each swimmer's race history, recent patterns, and the opposing team's coaching style.

{# stacked dice — each die in its own so rect + dots tilt together #}

SIMULATE THE MEET

For every plausible lineup your team could run, the optimizer races about 10,000 simulated meets. Each event is scored 5-3-1 and every swimmer races with their historical mean and variance.

{# shield with check #}

HEDGE FOR SURPRISES

Coaches don't always play their best lineup. The optimizer also weighs cases like reusing last week's lineup or countering your entries, so the result holds up if the opponent does something unexpected.

  • 5 years of NVSL meet results (2021 to 2025) scraped from mynvsl.com. Every swimmer's time in every event.
  • Your team's SwimTopia roster if available, which gives the authoritative current-season roster with correct ages and gender.
  • Optional time-trial PDF from HY-TEK Team Manager top-times export. Useful when you have new swimmers without race history.
  • Per-team coach style, measured as historical lineup consistency (do they repeat lineups week to week, or adapt every meet?).
  • Headline win probability. Your chance of winning the meet against a realistic mix of opponent lineups, calibrated to remove the optimizer's natural over-confidence.
  • Per-event expected points, accounting for placement uncertainty.
  • Up-arrow markers indicating swimmers being swum up an age group.
  • Side scenarios showing how the lineup performs against the opponent's best independent lineup, their last-week actual lineup, and their best possible counter.
  • Absences. The optimizer doesn't know who'll miss the meet. You mark absences manually in setup (Step 2).
  • Opponent prediction accuracy. The model picks the exact opponent lineup about 53% of the time, which is good enough to make smart decisions when combined with the multi-scenario hedge.
  • Week 1 and pre-season. Predictions drop to about 33% accuracy because there's no current-season data yet. The lineup is still better than blind, but expect more variance.
  • Opponent rosters. Without their SwimTopia roster (which requires admin access), we infer who's on the team from race history. Aged-out swimmers and quitters can occasionally appear.
